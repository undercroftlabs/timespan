## Introducing Timespan

One of my projects is **Timespan**, a TypeScript library for working with dates, durations, and intervals. JavaScript's `Date` object is useful, but measuring elapsed time, parsing duration strings, and accounting for calendar differences can lead to repetitive code. Timespan began as a way to simplify those tasks. It represents the interval between two dates and provides a consistent way to inspect, calculate, and express it. The first version covers duration parsing, interval calculations, and calendar-based breakdowns. It's a small project with plenty of interesting edge cases—dates have a way of providing those.
