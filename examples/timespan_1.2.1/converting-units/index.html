Convert a duration

The default span is 90 days from 15 January 2024. toDays() counts all 90 days. toTimeframe() splits the same interval from the largest unit, so those 90 days are 2 months, 4 weeks, and 2 days. Totals are whole units. toUnit accepts the same names as the factories, including short forms such as hr. Months and years follow the calendar from the start instant.
